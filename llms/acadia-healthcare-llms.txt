## What an agent needs to know first

Acadia Healthcare publishes **no public API, no developer portal, and no machine-readable contract**.
As of 2026-08-29:

- `https://developer.acadiahealthcare.com` — **NXDOMAIN**. There is no developer portal host.
- `https://api.acadiahealthcare.com` — resolves by CNAME to `w5rrqr.usa-e2.cloudhub.io`
  (MuleSoft CloudHub, US-East-2). Every anonymous path returns **HTTP 404 with an empty body**,
  including `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, and every `/.well-known/*` path.
  A gateway is deployed; nothing on it is publicly reachable or documented.
- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, WSDL, `.proto`, MCP server, or A2A agent card exists on any
  Acadia Healthcare host.
- No `/llms.txt`, no `/.well-known/security.txt`, no `/.well-known/api-catalog`.

Any system-to-system exchange Acadia participates in — HL7/FHIR with health systems, X12 EDI with
payers, EHR interfaces — is arranged through direct contracts and is not published. Do not attempt
to call `api.acadiahealthcare.com`; there is no public listener and no credential issuance path.
